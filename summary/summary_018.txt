BUG: player's shadow missing for ~2 s after every battle (user report)
  Cause (from the recompiled guest code, not from the log): after a battle the invulnerability timer
  (PlyTask +116, -1 per tick, sub_823AF9A0) sets blinkArm (PlyTask +0x28C0) = 1 on odd ticks, and
  PlyTask__Draw skips the player's draw on an armed tick: on the X360 the player AND its shadow blink.
  Our interpolation hook (frame_interp.cpp, commit e414576 "soften the player light blink") held the
  chara alpha at 0.5 for the whole blink window; a translucent player casts no sun shadow -> no shadow
  until the timer ran out.
  Fix: src/engine/frame_interp.cpp PlyTask__Draw hook: the 0.5 hold applies only on the frames of the
  tick the game armed (window tick == TickCount()); FlushPlyBlinkWindow already restores it at the next
  tick. New cvar bd_ply_blink_steady (default false; true = old behaviour). docs/INSTALL-SWITCH.md row.
  Shared code (all platforms), only acts with interpolation on.
  Not verified on hardware. "Translucent = no shadow" is inferred from the user's symptom matching the
  blink window exactly; if the shadow is still missing after battle, that inference is wrong.
  Expected look now: player pulses opaque / half transparent for ~2 s, shadow present on the opaque
  ticks (flickers with it, like the console).
