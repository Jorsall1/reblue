SESSION 008 (2026-11-23): Switch 2 dynamic resolution: resizing every 3 s, then a crash
(logs Desktop\bluedragon\logs, reblue_002.log). Dynamic resolution stays ON (user's requirement).
CAUSE OF THE RESIZING
  The render height stepped down 36 lines every ~3 s, 1080 -> 648 in 33 s, never up. Its input was wrong:
  22.6 ms of GPU work a frame at 59.8 fps (135% of wall time). plume's 625/384 timestampPeriod correction (from
  plume-switch.patch) applied on Switch 2 too, so every OunceVK reading was 1.63x too long. Corrected: ~16.3 ms at
  1080, ~13.5 at 900, ~11.6 at 648 (budget 15.5).
CRASH (no rex_crash.log, nothing in rex_stderr.log; the log stops 1-2 s after the 12th surface rebuild)
  Found in the rebuild path: Video::SyncBackBufferSizeLocked recreated the back buffer but only cleared its own
  framebuffer cache. Framebuffers pairing the old back buffer with a depth surface stay in the depth surface's
  cache, keyed by the old plume::RenderTexture address. A new texture at a reused address then hits a stale
  framebuffer of a destroyed image at the old size. Likely cause, not proven on hardware.
